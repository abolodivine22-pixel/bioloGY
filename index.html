DNA the instruction book

DNA is a twisted ladder. Its rungs are pairs of bases, and the order of the bases is the code for building you.

Four letters, endless code

Adenine always pairs with thymine. Cytosine always pairs with guanine. That rule lets a cell copy DNA accurately.

Photosynthesis sunlight into sugar

Plants capture light and use it to turn water and carbon dioxide into food, releasing oxygen.

The overall reaction

6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

It happens in chloroplasts, where green chlorophyll absorbs light energy.
